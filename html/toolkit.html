Cyber Emergency Response Guide

Immediate step-by-step actions for common cyber incidents. India-specific reporting guidance included.

🚨 Trapped? Act in the first hour Call 1930 · Report at cybercrime.gov.in · Block phone at ceir.gov.in

⏱️ The first hour matters most. Fraud reported to 1930 quickly can often be frozen before it leaves the first account. Stay calm, act now, verify later.

📞 Money stolen — Call 1930

  • Call 1930 (24×7 national cybercrime helpline) immediately after any financial fraud.
  • Keep ready: transaction/UTR number, amount, time, and the phone number or account the fraudster used.
  • Then file the online complaint to complete the record.

🌐 Every case — cybercrime.gov.in

  • Choose “Report Financial Fraud” for money loss, “Report Other Cybercrime” for hacking, identity theft, or threats.
  • Attach screenshots, chat/phone numbers, transaction IDs — delete nothing.
  • Save the acknowledgement number — banks and police will ask for it.

📱 Phone stolen — ceir.gov.in

  • Find your IMEI first: phone box, purchase bill, or your Google/Apple device dashboard.
  • File a police complaint — CEIR needs the FIR/complaint number to block the phone.
  • Submit the block request, then ask your operator to block the SIM and watch for OTP misuse.

Important Disclaimer

This guide provides general educational information. It is not a substitute for professional legal, financial, or incident-response advice. For serious incidents, contact law enforcement and professional security firms immediately. Emergency numbers may change—always verify current contacts on official websites.

🎣 Phishing Attack Response

  1. 1

    Do Not Interact Further

    Do not click any links, download attachments, or reply to the message. Do not enter any credentials on pages you accessed via the suspicious link.

  2. 2

    Change Compromised Credentials Immediately

    From a clean, trusted device, change passwords for any accounts where you entered credentials. Use unique, strong passwords. Enable MFA if not already active.

  3. 3

    Report the Phishing

    India: Forward to reportphishing@cert-in.org.in and report at cybercrime.gov.in.
    Global: Report to APWG, Google Safe Browsing, or the impersonated organization's abuse email.

  4. 4

    Scan for Malware

    Run a full antivirus/anti-malware scan on the device used. If you downloaded and opened any attachment, assume compromise until scan completes clean.

  5. 5

    Monitor Accounts

    Check for unauthorized activity on email, banking, social media. Set up login alerts. Review account recovery options and connected apps.

If you entered banking credentials: Contact your bank immediately via their official fraud helpline (on back of card). Request card block/reissue if card details were entered.

💳 Financial Fraud Response

  1. 1

    Contact Your Bank Immediately

    Contact your bank or financial institution immediately using the official customer-care number from your bank's website, mobile app, or the back of your card. Explain the fraudulent transaction and request that the affected account, card, or payment method be blocked if necessary.

  2. 2

    Block Cards and Payment Methods

    Temporarily block or disable debit cards, credit cards, UPI accounts, or other payment methods involved in the fraud. Do not use phone numbers or links provided by the suspected fraudster.

  3. 3

    Report the Fraud

    India: Report financial cyber fraud through cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930 as soon as possible. Provide transaction details, screenshots, phone numbers, and other relevant evidence.
    Global: Contact your local cybercrime authority or your financial institution's fraud-reporting service.

  4. 4

    Preserve Evidence

    Keep transaction IDs, bank statements, screenshots, emails, messages, phone numbers, payment receipts, and other communication related to the fraud. Do not delete evidence until the investigation is complete.

  5. 5

    Secure Your Accounts

    Change passwords and PINs associated with affected financial accounts. Enable multi-factor authentication where available and review recent login and transaction activity for unauthorized actions.

If money was transferred: Contact your bank immediately and provide the transaction/reference number. The sooner the fraud is reported, the sooner the bank or payment provider can attempt to stop or trace the transaction.

🔐 Hacked Account Response

  1. 1

    Secure the Account Immediately

    If you can still access the account, change the password immediately from a trusted device. Use a strong, unique password that you have not used on other websites.

  2. 2

    Sign Out of Unknown Devices

    Check the account's active sessions, logged-in devices, and recent login activity. Sign out of devices or sessions that you do not recognize.

  3. 3

    Enable Multi-Factor Authentication

    Turn on multi-factor authentication (MFA) or two-factor authentication (2FA). Prefer an authenticator app or security key when available.

  4. 4

    Check Recovery Information

    Review the account's recovery email address, phone number, security questions, connected applications, and forwarding rules. Remove anything that was added without your permission.

  5. 5

    Recover the Account

    If you cannot log in, use the platform's official account-recovery page. Do not use recovery links sent by unknown people through email, SMS, or social media.

If the same password was used elsewhere: Change that password on every other account where it was used. Never reuse a compromised password.
If the account was used to contact others: Inform your contacts that the account was compromised so they do not trust suspicious messages or links sent from it.

🦠 Malware / Ransomware Response

  1. 1

    Disconnect the Infected Device

    Disconnect the affected device from the internet by turning off Wi-Fi or unplugging the network cable. If ransomware is spreading across a network, disconnect the device from shared networks and drives.

  2. 2

    Do Not Interact With Suspicious Files

    Do not open suspicious files, click unknown links, or continue running programs that appear malicious. Do not attempt to modify or delete encrypted files if ransomware is suspected.

  3. 3

    Report the Incident

    India: Report serious cyber incidents through cybercrime.gov.in and contact CERT-In when applicable.
    Organization: Inform your organization's IT or security team immediately.
    Global: Contact your local cybercrime or cybersecurity incident-response authority.

  4. 4

    Scan and Clean the Device

    Use a trusted, updated antivirus or anti-malware solution to scan the device. If the infection cannot be removed safely, seek professional technical assistance instead of continuing to use the device.

  5. 5

    Restore From Safe Backups

    If files were encrypted or deleted, restore them from a known-clean backup after the system has been secured. Do not connect backup drives until you are confident the malware has been removed.

If ransomware demands payment: Do not assume that paying guarantees recovery of your files. Preserve evidence, disconnect affected systems, and seek assistance from cybersecurity professionals or relevant authorities.

🪪 Identity Theft Response

  1. 1

    Secure Your Accounts

    Immediately change passwords for email, banking, social media, and other important accounts. Use unique passwords and enable multi-factor authentication wherever possible.

  2. 2

    Contact Financial Institutions

    Contact your bank, credit-card provider, or other affected financial institutions using official contact information. Inform them that your personal information may have been misused.

  3. 3

    Check for Unauthorized Activity

    Review bank accounts, credit-card statements, emails, mobile accounts, and other services for transactions, account changes, or applications that you did not authorize.

  4. 4

    Report the Identity Theft

    India: Report the incident through cybercrime.gov.in and contact the relevant financial institution or authority.
    Global: Report the incident to your local cybercrime authority and affected organizations.

  5. 5

    Protect Your Personal Information

    Avoid sharing identity documents, OTPs, passwords, PINs, or financial information with unknown people. Be alert for follow-up phishing attempts using the information already obtained by the attacker.

If identity documents were misused: Contact the relevant issuing authority and affected organizations to determine whether the document or account needs to be blocked, replaced, or flagged.

📱 Stolen Device Response

  1. 1

    Lock the Device Remotely

    Use the official device-finding service, such as Find My Device (Android) or Find My (iOS), to remotely lock the device. If available, display a message or contact number on the lock screen.

  2. 2

    Track the Device Safely

    Check the device's last known location through the official tracking service. Do not attempt to confront or physically recover the device yourself if its location is known.

  3. 3

    Report the Theft

    Report the stolen device to the local police and provide the device's identifying information, such as the IMEI number when applicable. In India, you can also use the CEIR portal for reporting and blocking a stolen mobile device.

  4. 4

    Contact Your Mobile Network Provider

    Inform your mobile network provider about the theft and request that the SIM/eSIM be blocked or replaced. Ask about the procedures for protecting your mobile number from unauthorized use.

  5. 5

    Secure Your Accounts

    Change passwords for important accounts that were accessible from the stolen device, especially email, banking, cloud storage, and social-media accounts. Sign out the stolen device from your accounts where possible.

If the device contains sensitive information: Use the official remote-wipe feature if recovery is unlikely and the device contains sensitive personal, financial, or confidential information.
If banking apps were installed: Contact your bank immediately through its official channel and inform them that the device has been stolen. Monitor your accounts for unauthorized activity.
