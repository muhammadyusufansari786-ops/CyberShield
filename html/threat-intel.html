Where This Data Comes From

Every entry on this page is fetched from an official public feed by the CyberShield server, normalized, and cached in the project database. No sample, demo, or generated vulnerabilities are shown here.

  • CISA KEV (catalog) — vulnerabilities confirmed as exploited in the wild, with remediation due dates and known ransomware use.
  • NIST NVD (nvd.nist.gov) — CVEs published within the last 14 days, including CVSS scores when NVD has assessed them.

Honest severity: a severity level appears only when it comes from an NVD CVSS score. KEV entries without a CVSS are labelled “Actively exploited” instead of having a severity guessed for them.

The server refreshes the KEV catalog every 3 hours and the NVD feed every 90 minutes; each page load is served from the cached data, so the sources are never hammered. CISA is the only source of the “actively exploited” flag. Not affiliated with CISA or NIST — always confirm details against the linked advisory.
