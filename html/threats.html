Key Takeaways

Remember these core principles to stay safe

🤔

Think Before You Click

Verify links, sender addresses, and requests before taking action. Urgency is a red flag.

🔐

Use Strong, Unique Passwords

Password manager + MFA = best defense against credential theft.

🔄

Keep Everything Updated

OS, apps, firmware, antivirus. Patches fix vulnerabilities attackers exploit.

💾

Backup Regularly

Offline backups are your ultimate recovery from ransomware and data loss.

🎓

Stay Informed

Threats evolve. Follow trusted sources (CERT-In, CISA, Krebs) for updates.

📞

Report Incidents

Report cybercrime at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930 (India). Don't stay silent.
