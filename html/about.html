About CyberShield

Your beginner-friendly cybersecurity awareness platform. Empowering you to stay safe online through education, practice, and community.

Our Mission

CyberShield was created to make cybersecurity accessible to everyone. In an increasingly digital world, understanding basic security concepts shouldn't require a computer science degree. Our platform provides free, interactive tools and educational content to help you:

  • Build strong, unique passwords and manage them securely
  • Recognize phishing, smishing, quishing, and social engineering attacks
  • Understand common threats like ransomware, malware, and identity theft
  • Know exactly what to do if you fall victim to a cyber incident
  • Develop daily security habits that become second nature

Important Disclaimer

CyberShield is an educational platform designed for cybersecurity awareness. It is not a substitute for professional incident-response advice, legal counsel, or official law-enforcement procedures.

  • All interactive tools (password checker, quiz, simulator) run entirely in your browser. No data is stored on a server, logged, or transmitted.
  • Quiz questions, news items, threat descriptions, and glossary definitions are provided as sample content. They can be updated by editing the respective data files.
  • Emergency-response steps reference Indian authorities (CERT-In, cybercrime.gov.in, helpline 1930). Always verify with the latest official guidance.
  • The site does not collect personal information, nor does it ask you to submit passwords, bank details, or any personally identifiable data.
  • For serious incidents, contact law enforcement and professional security firms immediately.

Privacy & Data Practices

  • No tracking: No analytics, cookies, fingerprinting, or third-party scripts that track you.
  • Local-only storage: Progress tracking (quiz scores, checklist completion, favorites) uses your browser's localStorage only. Nothing leaves your device.
  • Password privacy: The password checker analyzes input entirely client-side. Your password is never sent over the network, logged, or stored.
  • No accounts required: Use all features immediately without registration.
  • Open data: All content data (quiz questions, news, threats, tips, glossary, resources) is stored in JSON files that can be inspected and modified.
